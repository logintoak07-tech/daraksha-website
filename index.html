FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Daraksha Overseas LLP do?

We are an international human capital and global placement enterprise that acts as a high-trust sourcing partner for major manpower agencies and foreign employers, managing candidates from sourcing through to flight dispatch.

Which kinds of candidates can you supply?

We maintain an active database across white-collar roles (engineering, IT and corporate, healthcare and hospitality) and blue-collar trades (construction and infrastructure), and we specialise in professional driver recruitment.

How are candidates verified?

Health clearances are conducted exclusively through GAMCA-approved and trusted medical laboratories, and trade skills are tested hands-on at professional testing centres supervised by certified industry experts. Drivers are additionally checked on licence, defensive driving and practical road tests.

How quickly will we receive candidates?

Initial candidate shortlists are delivered within 48 hours of requirement posting.

Does your process cover legal and travel formalities?

Yes. Our workflow covers employment contracts, salary structures, work visas, pre-departure briefing, ticketing, PoE clearance and airport dispatch.

Who will we be working with?

Our Designated Partners and Chief General Manager oversee operations directly, supported by dedicated HR, logistics and dispatch, and documentation and quality teams.
